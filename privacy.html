Privacy Policy

Hoby keeps the cat journal on this device. There is no account, no login, and no cloud backup.

Data stored on the device

Hoby stores cat names, meeting places, dates, notes, photo files you attach, draft pages, finished journal pages, and the local record of verified Gift Pack or Treasure Chest purchases. Preferences such as whether you finished the start screen are stored on the device as well. Clear All Data deletes these.

Tracking

The first time the journal home appears, Hoby asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. This version does not use the result. Allowing or refusing tracking does not change your journal or the Demo list. Hoby does not read an advertising identifier and does not send tracking data off the device.

Notifications

On that same first visit, after the tracking dialog closes, Hoby asks for notification permission. This version does not send notifications. Allowing or refusing does not change the journal.

Camera, photos, and microphone

The camera is requested only when you tap Take Photo, so a cat photo can be placed on the journal page. Photo library reading is requested only when you tap Import from Photos. Adding to your photo library is requested only when you tap Save on a finished page. The microphone is requested only when you tap Dictate Notes, so a name, place, or note can be turned into text you confirm. If you refuse, you can keep typing, use another photo path, or leave the finished page inside the app. Hoby does not upload the photo, the recording, or the transcript.

Purchases

Payment is handled by Apple. Hoby stores the verified transaction identifier on this device so the same purchase is not applied twice. That ledger is removed by Clear All Data or by deleting the app, and it cannot be restored.

Demo content

Demo encounters are written into the app. They are not collected from you and are not personalized with the tracking choice.

Contact

No support email is set in the product document. Privacy questions stay inside the in-app support text.
