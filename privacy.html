Privacy Policy

Hoby keeps the cat journal on this device. There is no account, no login, and no cloud backup.

Data stored on the device

Hoby stores cat names, meeting places, dates, notes, photo files you attach, draft pages, finished journal pages, and the local record of verified Gift Pack or Treasure Chest purchases. Preferences such as whether you finished the start screen are stored on the device as well. Clear All Data deletes these.

Tracking

The first time the journal home appears, Hoby asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. This version does not use the result. Allowing or refusing tracking does not change your journal or sample content. Hoby does not read an advertising identifier and does not send tracking data off the device.

Notifications

On that same first visit, after the tracking dialog closes, Hoby asks for notification permission. An optional evening journal reminder can be enabled in Settings. These reminders are scheduled locally on your device; Hoby does not use a remote push server. Refusing notification permission does not prevent journaling.

Camera, photos, and microphone

The camera is requested only when you tap Take Photo, so a cat photo can be placed on the journal page. Photo library reading is requested only when you tap Import from Photos. Adding to your photo library is requested only when you tap Save on a finished page. The microphone is requested only when you tap Dictate Notes, so a name, place, or note can be turned into text you confirm. If you refuse, you can keep typing, use another photo path, or leave the finished page inside the app. Hoby does not send your journal photos or notes to a developer-operated server. Dictation uses Apple’s Speech framework; audio may be processed by Apple’s speech recognition service under Apple’s privacy terms. Hoby does not require a developer account or cloud journal service.

Purchases

Payment is handled by Apple. Hoby stores the verified transaction identifier on this device so the same purchase is not applied twice. That ledger is removed by Clear All Data or by deleting the app, and it cannot be restored.

Sample content and third-party services

Sample cat entries are included for illustration and are not collected from you. Apple processes payments and may process speech recognition. Opening these legal pages connects to GitHub Pages, which may receive normal connection information such as an IP address. Hoby does not use a third-party generative service to render journal pages.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact provided on Hoby’s App Store listing. You can delete locally stored journal content and the local purchase ledger with Clear All Data. Copies already saved to Photos must be deleted separately in Photos.
