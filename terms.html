Terms of Use

These terms cover Hoby, an on-device cat photo journal. Hoby has no account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.

The journal

You can take or import a cat photo, write a name, place, date, and notes, preview a page, and then bind that page into the journal stored in this installation. A preview does not bind the page and does not use a purchase. Binding happens only after you confirm, and only when a Gift Pack or Treasure Chest is already on this device.

Gift Shop

Hoby sells consumable Gift Packs and Treasure Chests through Apple In-App Purchase at these prices: $0.99, $2.99, $9.99, $19.99, $29.99, $49.99, $69.99, and $99.99. The shop shows the pack or chest name and Apple’s price. It does not show a point or credit balance. Only verified transactions are accepted. A cancelled or unverified purchase adds nothing. Purchases stay on this device and cannot be restored after you remove the app or use Clear All Data. A refund removes only an unused pack or chest. A journal page that was already bound stays in the app.

Demo encounters

The Demo tab shows scripted cat encounters marked Demo. They are not other people’s accounts, and allowing or refusing tracking does not change their order or content.

Your content

Photos and text you add stay in the app’s private storage unless you tap Save to Photos. Saving asks for photo-library access and, if you allow it, writes a copy to your library. The copy in Hoby remains until you delete that page or clear all data.

Clear All Data

Clear All Data erases journal pages, drafts, photos stored by Hoby, the local purchase ledger, and the permission prompts’ saved state, then returns you to the start screen. This cannot be undone. There is no account to delete because Hoby does not create one.

Contact

The product document does not provide a support email. Support text is shown inside the app and does not open a browser.
