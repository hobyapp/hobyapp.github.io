The journal

You can take or import a cat photo, write a name, place, date, and notes, preview a page, and then bind that page into the journal stored in this installation. A preview does not bind the page and does not use a purchase. Binding happens only after you confirm, and only when enough Cat Credits are available on this device.

Gift Shop

Hoby sells consumable Gift Packs and Treasure Chests through Apple In-App Purchase at these prices: $0.99, $2.99, $9.99, $19.99, $29.99, $49.99, $69.99, and $99.99. The shop shows the pack or chest name and Apple’s price. The header shows your Cat Credit balance. After completing the initial permission steps, you may claim a free New User Gift Pack containing 10 Cat Credits once per installation. Credits are added only when you tap Claim. Only verified transactions are accepted. A cancelled or unverified purchase adds nothing. Purchases stay on this device and cannot be restored after you remove the app or use Clear All Data. A refund removes unused credits attributable to that purchase. A journal page that was already bound stays in the app.

Sample content

Sample cat entries are illustrative content, not other people’s accounts or a live community feed. Hoby provides Journal, Map, and Settings tabs.

Your content

Photos and text you add stay in the app’s private storage unless you tap Save to Photos. Saving asks for photo-library access and, if you allow it, writes a copy to your library. The copy in Hoby remains until you delete that page or clear all data.

Clear All Data

Clear All Data erases journal pages, drafts, photos stored by Hoby, the local purchase ledger, and the permission prompts’ saved state, then returns you to the start screen. This cannot be undone. There is no account to delete because Hoby does not create one.
